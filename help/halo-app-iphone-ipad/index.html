Help for GCU Halo

Halo GCU app for iPhone and iPad: what works

Halo is a website, halo.gcu.edu, and it works in Safari or Chrome on an iPhone or iPad. For an app-like icon, open it in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen. If GCU publishes an official app in the App Store, that one is safe to use; be careful with any other app that asks for your GCU password.

Put Halo on your Home Screen

  1. Open Safari and go to halo.gcu.edu, then log in.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Now Halo opens with one tap, like an app.

Tips for phones and iPads

  • Uploading work: save your file to the Files app (or iCloud Drive) first, then pick it from there in Halo's upload.
  • A page looks broken on iPad: try Safari's "Request Desktop Website" from the page menu (aA), or rotate to landscape.
  • Quizzes and timed work: use a computer if you can. A phone call, a notification or a lost connection mid-quiz is a bad time to find out.
  • Your password: only type your GCU password on GCU's own login page. No planner or study app needs it.

Where Halo+ fits

Halo+ works on iPhone and iPad as a Home Screen app, with notifications for what's due (on iPhone, notifications need it added to the Home Screen, which the app walks you through). The sync bookmark works on a phone too: you add it once and tap it while you're logged in to Halo in Safari. Halo sync is part of Plus ($4.99 a month); every new account gets Max free for 7 days.

Questions

Is there an official Halo app for iPhone?
Halo runs in the browser at halo.gcu.edu and works on iPhone and iPad. Check the App Store for apps published by Grand Canyon University itself; anything else is not from GCU.
How do I get Halo on my iPhone Home Screen?
Open halo.gcu.edu in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Can I take Halo quizzes on my phone?
You may be able to, but a computer is safer for timed quizzes: a call, a notification or a dropped connection can interrupt you.
