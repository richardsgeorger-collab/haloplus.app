Questions

Does it need my GCU password?
No, and it never asks. The Chrome extension reads only your own classes, grades and announcements, and only while you’re already logged in to Halo in that browser. It sends them to your own Halo+ account, and its code is public so anyone can check.
Is this from GCU?
No. Halo+ is an independent planner made by a student, not affiliated with Grand Canyon University.
What happens to my data?
It is yours. It is never sold or shared with advertisers, and you can export or delete all of it any time.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. Add it to your Home Screen and it works like an app.

Halo+ is independent and not affiliated with GCU. No GCU marks are used.
